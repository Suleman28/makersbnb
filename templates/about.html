About MakersBnB

MakersBnB is a place to list a spare room, a cabin, or a whole home - and to find somewhere characterful to stay. Hosts set their own nightly price and availability, guests request the dates they want, and every booking is confirmed by a real person.

List your space

Add photos, a description, your nightly rate and the dates you're free. Your listing goes live straight away.

Request a stay

Pick your dates on the calendar and send a request. Dates already booked are blocked out, so you'll never double-book a host.

Hosts stay in control

Every request goes to the host to approve or deny. Nothing is confirmed until they say yes.
